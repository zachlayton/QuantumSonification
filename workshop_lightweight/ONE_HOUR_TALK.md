# Hear a Qubit — one-hour workshop cut

## The promise

By the end of the hour, the audience should be able to hear and explain three
ideas:

1. A qubit contains more information than a single measurement probability.
2. Gates transform a state; they are not notes or sound effects by themselves.
3. Entanglement is revealed by joint correlations, not by listening to either
   qubit in isolation.

Everything else in the repository supports those ideas but is not required in
the main talk.

## The one-sentence framing

Quantum states do not have an intrinsic sound: we choose an explicit mapping,
then use listening to discover what that mapping reveals and what it hides.

## Run of show

### 0:00–0:04 — Cold open: move a state and hear the field change

Open:

```text
../BLOCH_HARMONICS/bloch_harmonics_conference_demo_v1/
QMW_Bloch_Harmonics_Conference_Demo_v1.maxpat
```

Start the audio before introducing notation. Move the Bloch vector through:

1. north pole,
2. equator at φ = 0°,
3. equator at φ = 90°,
4. south pole.

Ask only: “What changed, and what stayed the same?”

Do not explain spherical-harmonic normalization or all fifteen channels yet.
The opening is an experience, not a derivation.

### 0:04–0:15 — One qubit: probability and phase

Stay in the same conference patch.

- θ changes the balance between the computational-basis poles.
- φ changes phase while leaving the Z-basis probabilities unchanged.
- The visible harmonic bars are synthesis controls derived from the selected
  point; they are a mapping, not a literal quantum waveform.
- Pause at two equatorial points with identical `P(0)=P(1)=0.5` and different
  φ. This is the first important listening comparison.

Audience takeaway: equal Z-basis probabilities do not imply identical quantum
states.

### 0:15–0:26 — A circuit is a sequence of transformations

Open:

```text
../max/QMW_QAC_Circuit_Programmer_v1.maxpat
```

Use only these controls:

1. **CLEAR**
2. place or select **H**
3. **BELL**
4. **SEND QASM**

Build the Bell circuit slowly:

```text
H q0 → CX q0,q1
```

Explain `H` as preparing a balanced superposition and `CX` as correlating the
two qubits. Do not tour the full palette, sixteen time columns, GHZ preset, or
WEAVE preset.

Audience takeaway: the circuit is the recipe; the resulting state supplies the
data used by the sonification.

### 0:26–0:40 — Entanglement: local sameness, joint difference

Use the deterministic files generated by:

```bash
python quantum_sonification.py entanglement --shots 64 --seed 7
```

Play:

```text
generated_audio/two_qubit_measurements_plus_plus_64.wav
generated_audio/two_qubit_measurements_bell_64.wav
```

In both files, each qubit alone is approximately 50/50. In the stereo streams:

- `|++>` produces independent combinations `00`, `01`, `10`, and `11`.
- the Bell state produces only correlated `00` and `11` outcomes.

Play the pair twice. On the first pass, ask the audience to listen. On the
second, identify left and right as the two qubits.

Audience takeaway: entanglement lives in the relationship.

### 0:40–0:49 — Ideal model versus real IBM hardware

Open:

```text
../max/QMW_QAC_QFT_Spectral_Synthesizer_v5.maxpat
```

Do not submit a live job. Replay the saved local reference, then the completed
IBM archive:

```bash
python replay_local_pauli15_reference.py --preset bell --transform qft

python replay_ibm_pauli15_archive.py \
  ibm_results/d9hoc4bsbqfc73eqd0m0/job-d9hoc4bsbqfc73eqd0m0-pauli15-archive.json
```

Use only:

- the local/IBM source crossfade,
- the two wavetable views,
- the displayed Pauli/correlation values.

Ignore the QFT mode selector, four render modes, parameterized templates,
convolution controls, nonlinear/detail controls, and live-send controls.

The saved job completed on `ibm_kingston`; replay is deterministic and consumes
no additional IBM quota.

Audience takeaway: hardware results are not failed ideal states. They are
experimental data shaped by finite shots, compilation, layout, and noise.

### 0:49–1:00 — Questions and one return to the opening

Return to the single-qubit conference patch. Let one audience member choose θ
and φ, then ask:

1. What quantum information is controlling the sound?
2. What information does this particular mapping hide?
3. What would we map differently for another musical goal?

End on the distinction between **data**, **mapping**, and **sound**.

## What is deliberately cut

Keep these out of the main hour:

- package installation and IBM account setup,
- live IBM submission or queue monitoring,
- QFT/IQFT derivation,
- Pauli-15 tomography procedure,
- TEM and Fire Opal,
- reverb and impulse-response examples,
- four-qubit spatialization and HOA,
- GHZ and WEAVE circuits,
- all four V5 synthesis modes,
- density-matrix reconstruction details,
- OSC packet formats and bridge implementation,
- the mathematical derivation of degree-1 through degree-3 spherical
  harmonics.

These are follow-up material, not discarded material.

## Stage checklist

2. Confirm DSP is on and set a conservative master level.
3. Generate the deterministic entanglement files with the command above.
4. Replay the local and IBM archives once to populate both V5 buffers.

6. Keep the rendered WAV files available as the fallback if OSC or Max fails.
