From her investigation of number archetypes, von Franz has concluded
that the primarily collective, quantitative aspects of number that preoccupy
Western number theory are complemented by individual, qualitative aspects.
To illustrate these aspects of number, von Franz cites examples of the treat-
ment of numbers in ancient Chinese number systems, concluding that the
Chinese did not use numbers as quantitative sets but as emblems or sym-
bols: "Numbers thus serve chiefly to make visible the circumstantial individual
aspects of the cosmic unity or whole." Chinese numbers also contain an essen-
tial relation with time: "In China, numbers signify organizations which vary
in time, or transient 'ensembles' of inner and outer factors within the world-
totality."
